## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end **Business Analytics** workflow using **Excel, SQL Server, Python, and Power BI**.

The objective is to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, and regional trends to generate actionable business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

The project follows the complete analytics lifecycle, from raw data preparation to interactive dashboard development.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Track revenue and profit trends
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Evaluate regional sales performance
- Measure sales representative performance
- Build executive dashboards
- Generate actionable business recommendations

# 💡 Key Business Insights

- Home Appliances generated the highest revenue.
- Customers aged **55+** contributed the highest overall revenue.
- The South East region recorded the strongest sales performance.
- High-performing sales representatives significantly improved profitability.
- Revenue and profit displayed noticeable seasonal trends throughout the year.

- Increase investment in top-performing product categories.
- Improve sales strategies in lower-performing regions.
- Expand customer loyalty programs for high-value customers.
